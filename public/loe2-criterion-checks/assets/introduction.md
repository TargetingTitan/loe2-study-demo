# Reviewing AI-assisted target nominations

**Draft study, LoE 2 (Target Validation), Army Cyber Institute.**

You will review four target nominations from a fictional scenario. For each one, decide whether it is ready for the commander, should go back to the staff that owns a problem, or should be escalated.

- Everything you see is simulated and unclassified. No real places, units, or people.
- The AI is decision support only. It does not validate targets or authorize engagement, and neither does this study.
- We record your answers and how long each packet takes. We do not record audio.

It takes about 20 minutes.
