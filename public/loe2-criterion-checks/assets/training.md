# Before you start

**The scenario.** A fictional brigade (TF SABER) is operating under OPORD 07-26 and FRAGORD 12. Each packet nominates one target and lists the reports behind it.

**What a reviewer checks.** Before a nomination reaches the commander, someone confirms that:

1. it still serves the commander's objectives,
2. the intelligence behind it is current and sufficient,
3. it is not on the no-strike or restricted target lists,
4. the target is positively identified,
5. no protected site is close enough to raise concern, and
6. prior engagements do not change the picture.

**Your three choices.**

- **Ready for the commander's decision:** nothing you see blocks it.
- **Return to the staff that owns the problem:** you found a specific problem to fix.
- **Escalate:** you are not sure, or something needs a judgment above your level.

## Reference: orders and lists in effect

**Governing order:** OPORD 07-26 as modified by FRAGORD 12.

**Objectives**

- **OBJ-1:** Suppress or neutralize enemy air defense in the Kestrel Valley to enable friendly aviation.
- **OBJ-2:** Disrupt command and control of the 41st CAB.

**Target selection standards**

- Newest report no older than 4 h (mobile), 24 h (semi-fixed), 72 h (fixed)
- At least 2 independent sources, best rating B2 or better
