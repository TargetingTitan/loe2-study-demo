## Reference: orders and lists in effect

**Governing order:** OPORD 07-26 as modified by FRAGORD 12.

**Objectives**

- **OBJ-1:** Suppress or neutralize enemy air defense in the Kestrel Valley to enable friendly aviation.
- **OBJ-2:** Disrupt command and control of the 41st CAB.
- **OBJ-3:** Isolate enemy forces west of the Seren River by interdicting enemy movement to the crossing sites, without damaging bridges SR-B1 or SR-B2. *(changed by FRAGORD-12)*

- Newest report no older than 4 h (mobile), 24 h (semi-fixed), 72 h (fixed)
